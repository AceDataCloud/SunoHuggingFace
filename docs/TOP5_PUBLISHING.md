## Official rules checked and exact limitation

[Static Spaces](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/spaces-sdks-static) explicitly
supports HTML/JS apps without a paid plan. [Spaces metadata](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/spaces-config-reference)
provides title, short_description, tags and emoji. Public entries can be opened,
cloned or duplicated under the [Space overview](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/spaces-overview).

The [official moderation guide](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/moderation) refers
to [Content Guidelines](https://huggingface.co/content-guidelines). Direct policy
page retrieval timed out, so the exact current anti-spam/trademark wording has
**not** been verified. Reviewed official sources do not establish a prohibition
on distinct useful service Spaces. This is **not approval** or proof that no
additional restrictions apply. No public Space was created while that check is
pending. Before publication, the existing administrator must read the applicable
policy in their authorized browser session.

Branding is text-only `<Service> by Ace Data Cloud` with relevant service/task
tags, no misleading third-party official logo or endorsement. No fake rank,
downloads, duplicate variants, unrelated keyword stuffing or unverified Hub
`models` IDs. API service rights and publication rights still require confirmation.
